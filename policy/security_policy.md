# Sample Security Policy

These short rules are local training guidance for defensive email review.

- **R1** Never click links that use a raw IP address instead of a domain name.
- **R2** Report lookalike domains and brand-typo domains to security.
- **R3** Credential requests sent by email are always suspicious; verify through a trusted channel.
- **R4** Treat urgent deadlines and account-suspension threats as risk indicators.
- **R5** Do not enter passwords or payment details through links in unsolicited email.
- **R6** A sender and Reply-To domain mismatch requires independent verification.
- **R7** Only an explicit SPF, DKIM, or DMARC FAIL is evidence to consider; missing or unavailable results are informational only and are not risk indicators.
- **R8** Shortened links hide their destination and should be checked through approved tools.
- **R9** For screenshot-based reports, visible link text may differ from the actual URL.
